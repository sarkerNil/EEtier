HT1 / LT1 Grandmaster / Master

Reserved for elite editors demonstrating frame-perfect audio sync, pristine motion graphics, cinematic visual quality, and immaculate execution.

HT2 / LT2 Expert Tier

Tier 2 Masters

Top placement contenders with high dynamic range, clean velocity sync, polished color grading, and strong scene transition flow.

HT3 / LT3 Contender Tier

Tier 3 Ranked

Promising editors in the EE system with solid fundamentals, consistent beat sync, and growing creative storytelling abilities.
